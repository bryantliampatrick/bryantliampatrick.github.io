L.P. Bryant

I'm a Seattle-based archivist and technical art historian from San Diego, California. I'm also an independent researcher exploring information sensuality and invisible information labor.

I'm currently working with my good friend Nayan Saxena on connecting these areas to machine learning. I'm also currently assisting the Chihuly, Inc. archives.

If you have any questions at all, please feel free to get in touch, and check out more about me and my work below. Thanks for stopping by!



I believe that information is constructed, relational, and personal. Information work is necessarily social work.

Information can be joyful, painful, and enduring in equal measure. It is my job to honor the lives lived within that information.

I consult for United States-based repositories, both in English and American Sign Language. I also offer in-person work for Seattle-area organizations.

I use a sliding scale, relative to revenue streams and income sources (including pro bono work in many cases). I am also open to time-delimited work, such as last-minute or urgent requests, and grant-limited work.

I specialize in visual materials, (contemporary and historic photographic prints and negatives), rare photographic print processes, and complex records-papers collections. I am comfortable in corporate, non-profit, academic, and personal archives settings (including home visits).

If you're looking for a moving-image specialist, I would recommend checking with the Association of Moving Image Archivists for specialists in your area!
